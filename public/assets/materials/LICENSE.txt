The official ambientCG license page states that downloadable asset files are
provided under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal, including commercial use,
modification and redistribution without permission or mandatory attribution.

This project distributes only each set's 1024 x 1024 Color, NormalGL and
Roughness JPG images. They were recompressed at JPEG quality 87. No displacement,
DirectX normals, authoring files or preview renders are shipped. Level-specific
tints, repeating, roughness adjustments and original grime are applied in code.
Attribution is retained voluntarily. Materials are illustration assets, not
claims about the mechanical properties of real construction materials.
